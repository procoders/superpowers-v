## What this PR does

**What.** A tier (`standard` / `light`) can now name several `(backend, model)` pairs in
`.claude/compound-v.json`, and successive jobs of that tier are handed to them in weighted
manifest order, so one run spreads its work across several providers' quotas instead of draining
one. A manifest job opts in with the routing token `backend: pool`.

**Why.** Quota exhaustion on a single provider halts a run. Rather than react (retry/circuit-break),
this spreads load up front. It sits between the manifest (routing intent) and the backend-launcher
(execution): the manifest says `pool`, the run's `state.json` records the concrete pair, and every
downstream consumer — dispatch, retry, circuit breaker, `/v:status`, `/v:resume`, dashboard,
liveness — reads only the concrete pair, never the token.

**How.** Three layers. (1) `compound-v-project-config.py` gains `resolve_pools` /
`resolve_backend_max_parallel` warn-and-drop readers. (2) `compound-v-resolve-model.py` gains a pure
`resolve_pool(tier, index, stance, pools, ...)`; `compound-v-validate-manifest.py` gains a
`backend: pool` gate (worktree-only, no reviewers, no sensitive work, no `deep`, no `xhigh`, no
explicit `model`) plus an all-member resolution sweep. (3) The new `compound-v-pool-state.py`
freezes the expanded ring and per-job assignments into `state.json` once, and validates/returns them
on resume. `compound-v-failure-policy.py` learns to advance a quota-exhausted job to the next ring
member. Freeze itself is invoked by prose (`agents/parallel-dispatcher.md` Step 0a), not by a script.
