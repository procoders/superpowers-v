# v3.4.8 — a transient API failure is retried inside the workflow, and a reviewer Opus cannot serve is lifted, not lost (findings 118, 119) — design

**Probe (2026-09-03).** Three consecutive `API Error: 529 Overloaded` on the Opus reviewer (r8–r10 of v3.4.6), ~4 min in each; each
cost a run, a bookkeeping commit and a human relaunch. Compound V already owns the right policy — `compound-v-failure-policy.py`:
`overloaded` / `rate_limited` / `network` / `timeout` retry the same backend with exponential backoff + jitter, capped — but it is wired
into the residual subagent dispatcher and the external workers only. The emitted Workflow JS never loops `agent()`; a thrown transient
error becomes `status: error` and the wave is refused.

**Decision.**
1. **Classify, then retry in the workflow.** The emitted JS wraps every `agent()` call (implement, gate, record, finalize, review) in
   `withRetry(label, fn)`: on a thrown error whose message matches the transient classes (`529`, `Overloaded`, `rate limit`, `429`,
   `ECONNRESET`/`ETIMEDOUT`/`network`, `overloaded_error`) it waits the policy's backoff (`_backoff(attempt)` mirrored in JS: 2 s → 4 s →
   8 s… ± jitter, capped at 60 s) and retries up to `retry.max_attempts` (default 3). Any other error, and the last transient failure,
   propagate exactly as today. Every retry is logged to `<run>/logs/retries.jsonl` (label, attempt, class, wait) — measured, never estimated.
2. **Record the class honestly.** When retries are exhausted, Record writes `failure_class` from the classifier's class (`overloaded`,
   `rate_limited`, `network`, `timeout`), not `other`, so the retry/reroute policy and the dashboard see what happened.
3. **Lift the reviewer, never lower it.** A review job that fails transiently `retry.max_attempts` times on tier `deep` is re-spawned
   once on tier `frontier` (Fable) — the escalation the validator already accepts (3.4.6, finding 119) — and the receipt notes
   `escalated_from: deep`. Implementers are not escalated (their cap is turns, not capacity); external workers keep their own policy.
4. **Manifest knob, defaults on.** `retry: {max_attempts: 3, escalate_reviewer: true}` at the manifest top level (validator: ints 1..5,
   bool); absent ⇒ defaults. `max_attempts: 1` disables retries for a run.

**Files.** `scripts/compound-v-emit-workflow.py` (JS `withRetry` + the escalation branch + Record's class; selftests on the emitted
script strings and on a fake `agent()` that throws twice), `scripts/compound-v-validate-manifest.py` (`retry` block),
`skills/compound-v/execution-manifest.md` (the knob), `skills/compound-v/failure-policy.md` (one paragraph: the policy now applies on
Engine C too), `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Acceptance.** (1) A workflow whose `agent()` throws `529 Overloaded` twice then succeeds completes the job with two `retries.jsonl`
lines and no human step (fixture: a stub agent in the emitter selftest); (2) three throws ⇒ `status: error`, `failure_class: overloaded`;
(3) a deep reviewer exhausted on 529 is re-spawned on frontier once and its receipt says so; (4) `retry.max_attempts: 1` ⇒ no retry;
(5) validator refuses `max_attempts: 0`, `6`, `"3"`; (6) no timing claim anywhere — the backoff table is the policy's, the log is measured.
