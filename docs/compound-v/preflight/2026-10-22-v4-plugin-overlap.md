## Notes

1. The only real duplicates are the brainstorm shape and the Codex review runner. The first is deliberate, since v4 must stand alone. For the second, depend on nothing: the `CX` companion needs Node, its own broker and its own job store, and cannot take a snapshot checkout.
2. Gaps the audit found in our plan:
   - the builder-check step (a fresh reader of the spec) from `SP7/brainstorming/builder-check-prompt.md`;
   - session-id filtering, which the Codex gate already has (our plan has it too, via `run.active`);
   - a warning in `/v:init` when `/codex:setup --enable-review-gate` is on, because two Stop-time reviewers will conflict.
3. Optional dependencies that are safe: `codex-cli` itself (already planned), Context7 and the bundled `run` and `plugin-authoring` skills. Everything else stays out.
4. Limit of this audit: bundled skill bodies are inside the 2.1.306 binary and were not read; verdicts for `code-review`, `simplify`, `security-review`, `run` and `plugin-authoring` rest on their registered descriptions.
